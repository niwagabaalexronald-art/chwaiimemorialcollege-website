Projects

Building for the next 90 years — campus development and community works

Development

Campus Infrastructure Projects

From our 1934 foundations, the college continues to grow. Here is where we have come from — and where we are going.

🏫
Completed

Six-Classroom Block (Luwangula Block)

A modern classroom block named in honour of our pioneering heritage, providing spacious, well-lit learning rooms for O-Level classes.

🏢
Completed

Administration Block & Main Hall

The heart of school administration, assembly and examinations — housing the head teacher's office, staff room and the main hall.

🛏️
Completed

Boys' & Girls' Dormitories

Separate boarding houses for boys and girls with resident wardens — giving learners from upcountry a safe home on the hill.

🍳
Completed

Kitchen, Dining & Sick Bay

A hygienic central kitchen and dining area serving balanced meals, alongside a sick bay with referral care at neighbouring Holy Cross Hospital.

📚
Ongoing

School Library Expansion

Expanding our library collection with new textbooks, revision guides and storybooks — inviting partners and alumni to donate books.

🔬
Ongoing

Science Laboratory Upgrade

Equipping the physics, chemistry and biology laboratories to full UNEB practical standards, with modern apparatus and reagents.

💻
Completed

ICT Laboratory & E-Learning

A computer laboratory with internet access for digital literacy, e-learning support and exam preparation resources.

⚽
Planned

Sports Ground & Pavilion

Developing our playing field and a small pavilion for games, athletics and inter-house competitions — the future home of Chwa II sports glory.

🎓
Planned

Alumni & Endowment Fund

A fund to support bursaries for orphans and vulnerable children — so that no bright learner is ever turned away for lack of fees.

Community

Community & Church Projects

🤲

Bursary Sponsorship Drive

Working with the Uganda Orthodox Church and well-wishers to sponsor needy students through secondary school.

🌱

School Farm & Gardens

Learners participate in gardening and small-scale farming — feeding the school and teaching enterprise skills.

🧹

Namungoona Community Clean-Ups

Our students regularly join community service days around Namungoona Hill, honouring our motto through service.
