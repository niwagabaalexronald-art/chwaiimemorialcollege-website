Academics

A rigorous Uganda National Curriculum with a culture of excellence

Programmes

Ordinary & Advanced Levels

We offer the national curriculum from Senior One to Senior Six, preparing learners for the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) and Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE).

📗

O-Level (S.1 – S.4)

A broad foundation across sciences, arts and humanities. Learners sit UCE at the end of Senior Four, with strong emphasis on literacy, numeracy and practical skills.

  • All compulsory subjects plus option groups
  • Weekly tests, termly exams & continuous assessment
  • Guidance and career talks from Senior One
📘

A-Level (S.5 – S.6)

Focused combinations leading to UACE and university admission. Learners choose the combination that best fits their career path.

  • Science combinations (PCM, PCB, BCM…)
  • Arts combinations (HED, LGP, HEL…)
  • University application & scholarship guidance
