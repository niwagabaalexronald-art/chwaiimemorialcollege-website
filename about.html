About Us

Who We Are

A School Born of Vision and Royal Generosity

Chwa II Memorial College is a co-educational boarding and day secondary school in Namungoona, Rubaga Division, Kampala. It stands on Namungoona Hill, opposite Namungoona Police Post, within the mission centre of the Uganda Orthodox Church — a gift of land from Ssekabaka Daudi Chwa II of Buganda to support the Church's evangelistic and educational work.

Established in 1934 by the Rev. Reuben Spartas (Ssebanja Mukasa) and the Orthodox pioneers, the college began as a grammar school — the educational headquarters of the young Church — and grew into one of Uganda's most historic secondary schools. It is widely regarded as the first private secondary school in Uganda.

Today we serve learners from all backgrounds — many from humble families — keeping education affordable and accessible while insisting on excellence in academics, discipline and character.

Our Story

Milestones Through the Years

1924 — The Royal Gift

Kabaka Daudi Chwa II of Buganda donates land at Namungoona to Bishop Christopher (Reuben Spartas) for the Orthodox mission's evangelistic works.

1934 — The College is Born

Rev. Spartas founds the grammar school at Namungoona as the educational headquarters of the Uganda Orthodox Church — the seed of today's Chwa II Memorial College.

Mid-20th Century — Growth Amid Hardship

Through the turbulent 1950s–1980s the school endures political instability, yet continues to educate young Ugandans, including future church leaders such as Metropolitan Theodoros Nankyama, who served here as priest and head teacher.

Late 1980s–2000s — Renewal

Under Bishop (later Archbishop) Yonah Lwanga's leadership, the school booms to promising heights with new classroom blocks, dormitories and a science programme.

Today — A Living Legacy

Chwa II Memorial College continues to offer quality O and A Level education, producing leaders in the Church and nation — including Metropolitan Jeronymos Muzeeyi and Bishop Nektarios Kabuye, both alumni.

Governance

Administration

The college is administered under the Holy Metropolis of Kampala (Uganda Orthodox Church) through a Board of Governors, working with the school administration, teaching and non-teaching staff, the PTA and the students' body.

👤

Head Teacher

Provides overall academic and administrative leadership of the college.

👥

Board of Governors

Oversees policy, planning and the stewardship of the school's resources.

⛪

School Chaplain

Guides the spiritual life of the school community through worship and counsel.
