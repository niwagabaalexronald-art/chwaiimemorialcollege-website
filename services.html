Our Services

Everything your child needs to learn, grow and thrive

Student Life

What We Offer

🏠

Boarding Facilities

Safe and supervised dormitories for boys and girls with resident matrons and wardens, clean water, and a disciplined daily routine.

🍲

Feeding Programme

Balanced, affordable meals prepared in our school kitchen, with a menu reviewed regularly for nutrition and student wellbeing.

⛪

Chaplaincy & Worship

Regular services, morning devotion and Christian Orthodox teaching that anchor the moral and spiritual life of the school.

📚

Library & Reading Room

A growing collection of textbooks, reference works and literature — open daily for study, research and quiet reading.

🔬

Science Laboratories

Equipped physics, chemistry and biology laboratories supporting practical lessons and UNEB examination requirements.

💻

ICT & Computer Studies

Basic computing classes and digital literacy support, preparing learners for a technology-driven world.

🩺

Health & Sick Bay

A school sick bay staffed for first aid, with referral links to Holy Cross Orthodox Mission Hospital — our neighbour on Namungoona Hill.

🧭

Guidance & Counselling

Trained counsellors support learners through academic pressure, personal challenges and career decision-making.

Beyond the Classroom

Co-Curricular Activities

⚽

Games & Sports

Football, netball, athletics and volleyball — with inter-house and inter-school competitions throughout the year.

🎭

Music, Dance & Drama

Our drama and choir groups perform at school and church events, celebrating culture and faith through the arts.

🦁

Clubs & Societies

Scripture Union, debate club, science club, writer's club and more — every learner finds a community to belong to.

Fees & Bursaries

Affordable, With a Heart for the Needy

True to our founding mission, most of our learners come from ordinary and poor families. We keep fees modest and operate bursaries and work-study arrangements for deserving students.

  • Flexible payment arrangements for parents
  • Bursaries for orphaned & vulnerable children
  • Sponsorship partnerships with the Church and well-wishers
💛 No determined child is turned away for fees alone. Talk to the bursar — we will find a way together.
