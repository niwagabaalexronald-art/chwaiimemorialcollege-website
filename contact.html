Getting Here

About 6.5 km from Kampala city centre along the Hoima Road. Turn off at Namungoona (near the Uganda Orthodox Church headquarters); the college sits on the hill, opposite the Police Post. The Kampala Northern Bypass also passes through Namungoona, making us easy to reach from all parts of the city.

🤝 Parents are warmly encouraged to visit during office hours. Appointments can also be made by phone.
Chwa II Memorial College
