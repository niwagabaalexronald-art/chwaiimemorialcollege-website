What Happens Next
1️⃣

Application Received

Our admissions office reviews your form and contacts you within a few working days.

2️⃣

Interview & Placement

The learner attends a friendly interview and placement assessment, especially for S.1 and S.5 entry.

3️⃣

Enrolment

Successful applicants receive an admission letter and pay a modest enrolment fee to secure the place.

📋 Required documents at enrolment: a photocopy of the learner's birth certificate or National ID, previous school reports, and two passport photos.
