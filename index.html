Why Chwa II?

A Legacy of Learning on Namungoona Hill

Founded by the Orthodox pioneers on land donated by Ssekabaka Daudi Chwa II of Buganda, our college blends academic rigour, strong moral formation and a proud heritage of producing leaders in the Church, government and society.

🏛️

Rich Heritage Since 1934

Nine decades of continuous education. Among our alumni are Metropolitan Jeronymos Muzeeyi of the Holy Metropolis of Kampala and Bishop Nektarios Kabuye of Gulu.

📚

Full O & A Level Curriculum

Uganda National Curriculum in Sciences, Arts and Humanities, taught by dedicated staff in a disciplined, faith-centred environment.

🤝

Moral & Spiritual Formation

Rooted in the values of the Uganda Orthodox Church: humility, hard work, service to others and respect for all — the true spirit of our motto.

Welcome Message

From the Head Teacher's Desk

Welcome to Chwa II Memorial College, Namungoona — a school where every learner is known by name and raised to aim high, whatever their background.

Our motto, “Through Difficulties to the Heights,” reminds us that great destinies are forged through perseverance. We serve learners from all walks of life, many from humble families, and we take pride in walking with each of them to the heights of their potential.

I warmly invite parents, guardians and well-wishers to partner with us. Together, we keep the flame of this historic college burning bright.

📌 Quick Facts

  • Boarding & day school, boys and girls
  • O-Level (S.1 – S.4) and A-Level (S.5 – S.6)

  • Chapel, library, laboratories & sports grounds
  • Located on Namungoona Hill, Kampala
  • Administered by the Uganda Orthodox Church
